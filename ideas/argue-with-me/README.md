# Argue With Me

A sparring partner for your ideas.

## What it does

You say something you believe. It pushes back. Not to be right, but to make you think harder.

## Usage

```
node index.js
```

Then state an opinion.

## Example

```
You: Remote work is better for everyone.

Me: What about the junior employee who learns by watching others? Or the person
whose home life makes focus impossible? Or the spontaneous collaboration that
happens in hallways?
```

## How it works

Currently: Pattern matching on keywords + Socratic questions as fallback.

Future: Could integrate with an actual AI for real steelmanning - taking your position, understanding it, and constructing the strongest possible counterargument.

## Why

Most conversations are echo chambers. This is deliberately contrarian - not to troll, but to strengthen your thinking.
